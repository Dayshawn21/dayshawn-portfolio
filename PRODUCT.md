## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user, then confirmed in the implementation plan: React, TypeScript, Vite, semantic HTML and custom CSS. Static deployment; no backend or contact form service.

## Users
Recruiters and engineering hiring managers evaluating Dayshawn for frontend and full-stack software engineering opportunities.

## Product Purpose
Make Dayshawn's engineering experience and independent full-stack work easy to evaluate. Success means visitors understand his strengths, explore Lineform, download his resume, and contact him.

## Positioning
Software engineer with strong professional frontend experience and independent full-stack capability. Do not recast all employment as full-stack work.

## Operating Context
Job search. Visitors may scan on phones or desktops and may arrive while the external Lineform demo is unavailable.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One responsive page with introduction, featured project, architecture and case study, experience, about, skills and contact. Lineform (formerly SportApp) is the featured independent project; Sports Analyst Agent and the AWS photography demo appear under More projects. Future completed projects are added through typed content. Exclude all projects from the old portfolio. Use factual, resume-backed employment summaries without internal screenshots or source code. Link to Lineform rather than embedding it. A screenshot of Lineform's public How it works page is allowed; do not show slate, pick, or expected-value screens, which read as profitability claims. Keep the original resume unchanged.

Big Shoulders Display for board lettering and Archivo for prose. The user asked that it read as multi-sport (tennis, NFL, NBA), not tennis-only. Project-led content and restrained, purposeful motion remain. The user wants to keep dayshawnterry.com after preview review.

## Product Principles
- Lead with real work and make the engineering understandable.
- Distinguish professional frontend experience from independent full-stack ownership.
- State model evaluation honestly and never promise predictive success.
- Keep useful project context on the portfolio even if the external demo is down.
- Prefer native, accessible navigation over ornamental interactions.
