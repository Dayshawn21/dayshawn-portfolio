## Stack

## Users
Recruiters and engineering hiring managers evaluating Dayshawn for frontend and full-stack software engineering opportunities.

## Product Purpose
Make Dayshawn's engineering experience and independent full-stack work easy to evaluate. Success means visitors understand his strengths, explore SportApp, download his resume, and contact him.

## Positioning
Software engineer with strong professional frontend experience and independent full-stack capability. Do not recast all employment as full-stack work.

## Operating Context
Job search. Visitors may scan on phones or desktops and may arrive while the external SportApp demo is unavailable.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One responsive page with introduction, featured project, architecture and case study, experience, about, skills and contact. SportApp is the only independent project at launch. Future completed projects are added through typed content. Exclude all projects from the old portfolio. Use factual, resume-backed employment summaries without internal screenshots or source code. Link to SportApp rather than embedding it. A screenshot of SportApp's public How it works page is allowed; do not show slate, pick, or expected-value screens, which read as profitability claims. Keep the original resume unchanged.

## Brand Commitments

The current direction is a multi-sport stadium scoreboard: painted charcoal board, white enamel tiles, hand-painted gold, a red live lamp. Big Shoulders Display for board lettering and Archivo for prose. The user asked that it read as multi-sport (tennis, NFL, NBA), not tennis-only. Project-led content and restrained, purposeful motion remain. The user wants to keep dayshawnterry.com after preview review.
