## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a multi-sport stadium scoreboard: the work is posted, not pitched. It refuses the white editorial engineer page with aphorism headlines over a paragraph.

OWN-WORLD: Painted charcoal board ground (#15181c), white enamel tiles with board-ink letters, hand-painted gold numerals and rules, one red "live" lamp. Big Shoulders Display for everything set on tiles and boards; Archivo for prose and tables, tabular figures in fixed cells. Long-form content (pipeline, decisions, screenshot, now-posting) sits on bolted panels with flat rivet heads; the pipeline detail and About band are enamel; short hero copy is painted straight on the board. Square corners, rivets not shadows.

STORY: The visitor reads the name and line score (real Visualutions figures with source), sees SportApp posted across Tennis, NFL, NBA, explores the pipeline, reads the decisions, scans the experience box score, and contacts him.
